## Typical chamfers

- External thread cut with a die: 45° lead-in chamfer. Specify the exact width separately. [RUKO](https://ruko.de/de/wissen/how-to/wie-schneide-ich-ein-aussengewinde-in-2-schritten/)
- Internal thread before tapping: 90° countersink, equivalent to 45° on each side relative to the axis. The countersink diameter is slightly larger than the nominal diameter. [Gühring](https://guehring.com/produkte/gewindewerkzeuge/)

These are typical manufacturing recommendations. Pitch and chamfer width are different dimensions. Keep undimensioned chamfers marked as assumptions or unresolved items.
